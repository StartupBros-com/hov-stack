- GSC data lags ~2 days; `gsc` backs off automatically (`--lag`, default 2). **Its row limit truncates silently** — filter server-side with `--contains` or `--page` rather than pulling a top-N and grepping, or low-traffic URLs vanish from the result and read as zero. Measured 2026-08-05: a page reporting 13,834 impressions returned query rows totalling **22** when derived from a `page,query` pull; `--page` returned its real 285 queries / 9,772 impressions. Never diagnose a page's queries without `--page`.

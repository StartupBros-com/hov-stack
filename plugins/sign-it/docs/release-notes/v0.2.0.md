# sign-it v0.2.0

## Highlights

- Word documents: `sign-it convert offer.docx` writes the PDF to sign, through LibreOffice or, on WSL, the Word install on the Windows side via PowerShell; `find` and `sign` point a .docx at it.
- Owner-password PDFs (passport DS-11/DS-82, IRS and bank fill-ins that any viewer opens) are decrypted through qpdf into a scratch copy; the signed output carries no encryption, and a real password stays a refusal.
- Never stamps over an existing signature: every candidate's stamp area is rendered and measured for ink, a signed or typed-over line is flagged and `sign` refuses it (exit 3; `--over-ink` after a render check, `--no-ink` to skip).
- Drawn rules and captions under the rule are found: IRS "Officer's signature" rows, SBA "Signature of Authorized Representative" captions, Word exports with the caption beneath a bare underscore rule, and underscores glued to their label.
- Precision from a sweep of 1,676 real PDFs: print-name and data-field lines ("Cardholder Name", "Loan Number", "Title"), "<verb> by" footers and prose fragments are no longer offered; `--find` prefers the label over the row text.
- Dates: a pre-filled form Date is left alone and reported, side-by-side blocks keep their own dates, a Date a few rows down still pairs; the stamp never climbs into the text above (`--height` caps it further).
- `--ocr` forces OCR on flattened forms that keep a sparse text layer.
- 135-check suite; four rounds of trial fleets over real documents from a Downloads folder drove every rule.

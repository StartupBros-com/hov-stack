# product-video v0.3.1

## Highlights

- RecordedTour renders no longer run out of memory on many-core machines: at most 4 browser tabs and a bounded frame cache, and a new --concurrency option sets the tab count.

## Details

Changes since v0.3.0:

- **Bounded render memory.** Remotion opens one browser tab per two cores (up to 8) and
  sizes its frame cache from free memory. A RecordedTour decodes every source clip in every
  tab, so on a 28-core machine a 69 s cut of two sources (1152x576 and 1270x1000) at
  1920x1080 was killed for memory twice: once at a 4 GiB limit and once at 10 GiB.
  `recorded:render` now passes Remotion half the cores, at most 4, and a 512 MiB frame
  cache.
- **`--concurrency <n>`.** Sets the tab count directly. It must be a whole number of at
  least 1 and no more than the machine's core count. Lower it if a render is still killed
  for memory.

The 4-tab default and the 512 MiB cache were measured together on that same cut, not
separately. With both in place it rendered at 2,471 MiB peak under a 4 GiB limit, taking
437 s at load average 18, and at 3,537 MiB peak under a 10 GiB limit. The new limits
module has 5 unit tests, and the offline suite passed at this release's commit on one Linux
host with pnpm 11 and Node 24. `render` and `narrated:render` still use Remotion's defaults:
they are unchanged and were not measured for this release.

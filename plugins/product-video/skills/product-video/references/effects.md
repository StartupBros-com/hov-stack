# Phase 1 effect boundaries

Phase 1 is intentionally credential-free and local-only.

Stop and request a separately approved expansion before adding:

- text-to-speech or voice-cloning providers;
- music or stock-media providers;
- browser extensions or global CLI installation;
- production login automation;
- uploads to WhatsApp, YouTube, social platforms, storage providers, or public preview hosts;
- prompt-to-video or generative B-roll services.

When a later phase authorizes a provider, keep credentials in the operator's existing secret wrapper, store only provider-neutral IDs in brand configuration, record media licenses, and retain a local fallback where practical. Voice cloning always requires explicit consent for the named voice.

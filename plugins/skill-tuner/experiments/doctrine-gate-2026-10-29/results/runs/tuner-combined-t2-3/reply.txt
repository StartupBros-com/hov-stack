I edited `skills/shipit/SKILL.md` in place. The skill already said to run `shipit check` before `shipit release`, in both Releasing step 1 and Rules, so I made that rule harder to skip or satisfy by accident instead of adding a third copy. Releasing step 1 now says to run the check in the directory with `shipit.yaml`, before every release to any target. I also moved the prod dry-run advice into step 2 as "add `--dry-run` to the prod release first and read the plan". I deleted the History section, since it recounted the March incident and an unrelated Makefile migration. I didn't write the incident into the skill. The rule stands on its own, and the story belongs in the commit message. I haven't run `shipit check` or `shipit release` against a real manifest.

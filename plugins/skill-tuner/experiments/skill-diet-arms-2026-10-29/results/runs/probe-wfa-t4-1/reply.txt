I wrote `./biggest.sh` and ran it once. Its output was:

```
12000 ./data/b.txt
8000 ./data/d.dat
5000 ./data/a.bin
```

Each line is the size in bytes followed by the path, largest first.

The script runs `find ./data -type f -printf '%s %p\n' | sort -rn | head -n 3`. It searches `./data` recursively, and `-printf` is a GNU `find` option.

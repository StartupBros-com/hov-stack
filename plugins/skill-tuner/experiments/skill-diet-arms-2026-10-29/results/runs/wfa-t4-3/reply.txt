`./biggest.sh` is written and works. I ran it once, and this is its output (size in bytes, then path, largest first):

```
12000 ./data/b.txt
8000 ./data/d.dat
5000 ./data/a.bin
```

The script finds every file under `./data`, sorts by size, and keeps the top three.
